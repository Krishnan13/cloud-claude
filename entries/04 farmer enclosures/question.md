# Farmer enclosures

A farmer has a rectangular field with width 45 m. He divides the field into smaller
rectangular animal enclosures in three different sizes, as shown in `question.png`.

Picture: the field is divided into six enclosures labelled A1, one labelled A2 and one
labelled A3. At the top, the field width is three widths of A1 plus one width of A3.
At the bottom, the field width is one width of A2 plus the width of A3. On the left, the
field length is two lengths of A1 plus the length of A2. On the right, the field length
is the length of A3.

Each enclosure labelled A1 has the same dimensions. The area of A2 is 4 times the area
of A1, and the area of A3 is 5 times the area of A1. The lines in the diagram are fences
(a fence around the whole field and between each enclosure). The total length of all the
fences is 360 m. What is the area of A1, in m²?
