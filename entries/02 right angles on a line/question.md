# Right angles on a line

For some points of the form (k, 7), the points A(2, 3), B(12, 13) and (k, 7) form a
right-angled triangle. Suppose that C is such a point so that triangle ABC is right-angled
at C. Suppose that D is also such a point so that triangle ABD is right-angled at D
(D ≠ C). What is the area of ACBD?
