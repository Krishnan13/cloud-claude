\begin{center}{\Large\bfseries Eight Balls in a Row}\end{center}

\section*{Problem}

Eight balls stand in a row:

\begin{center}\small\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.6}\begin{tabular}{c|cccccccc}
\toprule
Position & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 7 & 8\\
Ball & cricket & football & volleyball & tennis & golf & baseball & rugby & bowling\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}

The volleyball has value 100, and the values of any three consecutive balls add up to 600. What is the value of cricket ball $+$ bowling ball?

\section*{Solution}

Name each ball by its position: $b_1$ is the value of ball 1, $b_2$ the value of ball 2, and so on. Then $b_3 = 100$, and we want $b_1 + b_8$. Balls are \emph{consecutive} when they stand next to each other with no gap. There are six groups of three consecutive balls: (1,~2,~3), (2,~3,~4), (3,~4,~5), (4,~5,~6), (5,~6,~7), (6,~7,~8). Each group adds up to 600.

\textbf{\boldmath Step 1: slide the group.} Compare the first two groups:
\[
\begin{array}{ccccccccc}
b_1 & + & b_2 & + & b_3 &   &     & = & 600\\
    &   & b_2 & + & b_3 & + & b_4 & = & 600
\end{array}
\]
Both totals are 600 and both contain $b_2 + b_3$, so $b_1 = b_4$.

\textbf{\boldmath When the group slides one place to the right, the ball that leaves and the ball that joins have equal values.}

\textbf{\boldmath Step 2: the row repeats.} The ball that leaves and the ball that joins are always 3 places apart. So $b_1 = b_4 = b_7$, $b_2 = b_5 = b_8$ and $b_3 = b_6$, and the row is the first three values repeating:

\begin{center}\small\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.6}\begin{tabular}{c|cccccccc}
\toprule
Position & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 7 & 8\\
Value & $b_1$ & $b_2$ & $b_3$ & $b_1$ & $b_2$ & $b_3$ & $b_1$ & $b_2$\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}

\textbf{\boldmath Balls 3 places apart have equal values, so $b_8 = b_2$.}

\textbf{\boldmath Step 3: the answer.} Using the first group and $b_3 = 100$:
\[ b_1 + b_8 = b_1 + b_2 = 600 - b_3 = 600 - 100 = 500. \]

\begin{center}\textbf{\boldmath Answer: cricket ball $+$ bowling ball $= 500$}\end{center}
